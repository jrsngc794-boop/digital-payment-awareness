04 — SAFETY FIRST

Payment Precautions

Follow these basic habits to make digital payments safer.

Protect Your Payment Information

Never share confidential payment credentials with anyone, even if someone claims to be from a bank or payment service.

⚠️ Important: You never need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
✓ Never share your UPI PIN, OTP or password.
✓ Verify the receiver's name and amount before paying.
✓ Avoid suspicious links and unknown applications.
✓ Be careful with fake customer-care numbers.
✓ Use official and trusted payment applications.
✓ Keep your phone and payment apps updated.
✓ Report suspicious activity to your bank/payment provider.
